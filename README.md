## How to read this list

- Archives follow announcement batches; first-submission and revision dates are recorded separately. PrismWAM in the October 21 archive is a supplemental review of the October 20 batch.
- New papers, substantive revisions, and metadata/format-only changes are distinguished so old results are not presented as new.
- Abstract screening is distinguished from selected full-text checks. An accessible repository is not proof of complete or reproducible code.
- Code/project status is historical as of the review recorded in each edition and may change later.
- There is no fixed inclusion quota. Scope and gaps are recorded per edition; October 19 does not claim complete cross-category coverage.

Papers, code, and projects remain the work of their respective authors and rights holders. Cite the original papers when referencing the research.
