Reading priorities: DreamFree and Asynchronous Robot Policies separate expensive prediction or semantic memory from fast control. FEIJOA and DiMuRe provide concrete grounding, skill verification and recovery evidence. AIRoA-0, GUILD, PyRUA-DataFarm and DreamX illustrate different data/transfer strategies whose success and cost metrics are not interchangeable.

This is not an exhaustive full-text review of all categories or of replacements outside cs.RO.

All numerical findings are author-reported and have not been independently reproduced. Distinguish simulated/offline results, physical closed-loop trials, partial-credit scores and subgoal success. Linked repositories or project pages do not establish complete, reproducible implementations.

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.05377v1 · New paper · First submitted 2026-11-03T18:59:56Z. Review: selected full-text methods and results checks
