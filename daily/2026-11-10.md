### Streaming Failure Forecasting: VLA Failure Monitors That Keep Learning After Deployment

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.11688v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-10 · First submitted 2026-11-08T23:17:09Z. Review: Full-text spot check of evaluation and limitations.

**Contribution:** SFF updates only a failure monitor's final linear layer after each outcome while keeping its backbone fixed.
