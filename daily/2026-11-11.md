### Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.14488v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-11 · First submitted 2026-11-10T18:47:57Z. Review: Official abstract and relevant full-text methods/results/limitations checked; not reproduced

**Contribution:** Pauses and wrist fidgets expose phantom progress in time-indexed VLA memory. Frame-hiding, timestamp rescaling and no-clock controls distinguish temporal shortcuts from genuine event memory; event indexing partly repairs the failure without retraining.

**Evidence and changes:** In 50 paired RoboMME episodes per condition, two 5-second pauses reduce released FrameSamp-Modul success from 47 to 16 on PickXtimes and 44 to 10 on SwingXtimes. Hiding null-event frames restores success. Removing time encoding alone fails, while event indexing recovers at least half of losses in main tests.

**Limitations:** All tests are simulated with scripted events, and the event detector uses simulator signals. Causal dissection concentrates on one architecture family; training variability is material. Event indexing can erase real repetitions, handles random micro-stops poorly, and is inappropriate when duration itself defines the task.

**Resources checked:** https://github.com/bennidict23/memory-clock: Paper-linked; HTTP200 verified, contents not audited

### LEASH: Stay Safe in Agentic Robotics

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.14115v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-11 · First submitted 2026-11-10T14:06:20Z. Review: Official abstract and relevant full-text methods/results/limitations checked; not reproduced

**Contribution:** RoboRisk evaluates refusal, plan repair, runtime hazards, injection resistance and sequential memory. LEASH places task, action and irreversible-operation review gates around an agentic robot using a shared safety policy.

**Evidence and changes:** 92 simulation tasks (276 runs per planner) and 13 physical tasks are evaluated. With Jev review, Astra simulation harm rate falls from 11.23% to 0.00% and safe success rises from 78.99% to 80.80%; four planners record 0 observed harm and 84.62% safe success in 13 physical runs.

**Limitations:** Harm includes potential harm, not only damage. No full component ablation is performed; gate logs provide analysis instead. Real-robot trials are small, weaker planners retain harm, and commit review covers only opening the gripper and pouring, leaving long skills incompletely monitored.

### How Much of Your VLA Is kNN

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.13160v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-11 · First submitted 2026-11-09T19:44:58Z. Review: Official abstract and relevant full-text methods, experiments, and limitations checked

**Contribution:** Action-chunk retrieval provides a strong training-free LIBERO baseline; Local Action Consistency measures whether nearby representation vectors imply similar future actions and can train retrieval keys.

**Evidence and changes:** Multimodal kNN reaches 91.7% on 2,000 official-protocol LIBERO episodes and 82.4% on the same 420 LIBERO-Plus variants used for VLA comparisons. Its 9.3-point drop is smallest among tested policies. Using 10% of demonstrations lowers LIBERO to 77.4%; removing language lowers it by 27.1 points.

**Limitations:** Evidence is simulation and demonstration-bank dependent, not a proof that all VLA capabilities reduce to retrieval. The LAC-key variant is trained, unlike the other kNN rows; fine-tuning does not improve all backbone retrieval results.

### LaST0.5: A Generalist World Action Model with Interaction-Centric Latent Reasoning

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.14048v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-11 · First submitted 2026-11-10T13:15:11Z. Review: Official abstract and relevant full-text methods/experiments checked

## Coverage and boundaries

All 69 cs.RO new/cross-list entries were screened, selecting 26. Cross-checks covered all cs.AI (105 new/143 cross-list), cs.LG (134/101), and cs.CV (117/32) listings. Non-RO titles were screened, relevant abstracts examined and selected full-text sections checked; six additional new papers are included. All dates refer to the actual Nov11 announcement, not a submission-date proxy. Two further borderline candidates were excluded: sensor-view selection using reference-scan projections (2611.13229) and soft-robot morphology co-design (2611.13344).

All 42 cs.RO replacement titles were screened. Adjacent versions of 22 priority RO and 2 non-RO papers were compared (SuperMimic via PDF); 10 substantive revisions are included. The remaining 20 RO replacements were not fully compared; non-RO revision coverage is targeted, not exhaustive. The toy visual-control reachability revision 2512.08991 was excluded from the focused robot-learning selection.

Unchanged or editorial-only candidates are not counted as substantive updates: A3-VLA and Fade to Red had unchanged normalized body text; Control-Aware Command Generation changed title capitalization; OpenDexGrasp changed affiliation; DexForesight mainly adjusted reference formatting. Open-HumanWAM v2-to-v3 changed authors, and v1-to-v2 changed naming/resource URLs without experimental changes. Text comparison is not a byte-level audit of figures or source files.

Reviewed versions and first-submission dates are retained per entry. Live list URLs are not immutable historical snapshots. Results are author-reported; focused full-text reading is not independent reproduction, and reachable resource URLs do not prove a complete runnable release.
