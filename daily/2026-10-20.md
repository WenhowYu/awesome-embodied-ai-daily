## Compile Once, Run Cheap: Frozen, Portable Skills for Embodied Agents, and What Recursive Self-Improvement Adds

- arXiv: [2610.25114v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25114v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-18 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** A strong model writes, repairs, and freezes skills offline for execution by code or a cheaper model. The work studies executable experience, validation, inheritance, and deployment cost.

**Evidence and limitations.** Three actually reused skills succeed on 148/150 new-layout trials using code only. Iterative repair beats rewrite in 8/8 comparisons, but only 5:3 after matching best-of-k selection opportunities. Three inherit-only chains suggest inheritance supplies most gains, with weak evidence for extra feedback benefit. Experiments are simulation-only, with readable success criteria, RGB-D, and calibration; there are no ASPIRE/CaP-X runs or cross-embodiment tests.

**Code and materials.** The public materials include a framework, seven frozen skills, experiment ledgers, and recomputation scripts. Raw model I/O, author sessions, and evidence videos were not public.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25114v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/Orillusion/embodied-skill-rsi)

## Where is Physical RSI capability from? An Observational Scaling Law of LLM-as-policy

- arXiv: [2610.26848v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26848v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-19 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** The study measures initial capability, improvement rate, success, and recovery separately over 18 RoboDojo tasks and up to 17 interaction rounds. History and workspace ablations probe which retained experience supports improvement.

**Evidence and limitations.** In six-task ablations, clearing history reduces late-stage success from 15/36 to 3/36, while workspace plus grader alone reaches 17/36. Raw image history is therefore not shown to be necessary. A frozen workspace reaches 25/60 on new layouts; cross-task evidence comprises only three examples. This is simulation-only, with one chain per task, stopping at first success and unequal model configurations/revision time budgets; scaling relationships are exploratory correlations.

**Code and materials.** The project linked a repository, but it was empty and had no usable code.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26848v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/PhysRSI-Eval/PhysRSI-Eval-code) · [Project](https://physrsi-eval.github.io/)

## SARAM: Sparse Adapter Allocation with Reuse and Merging for Lifelong Robot Learning

- arXiv: [2610.24804v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24804v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-17 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** SARAM reuses old LoRA adapters, sparsely allocates new ones, and penalizes redundancy. It identifies the task at each episode start and merges the selected adapter into the backbone, providing a parameterized lifelong-learning approach.

**Evidence and limitations.** LIBERO baselines were reproduced with the same backbone/training budget over three seeds. A seven-task UR5 stream ends at 94.3%, compared with SeqLoRA 14.3% and ER 41.4%, using about 50 demonstrations and 10 tests per task. Task identification and merging cost about 140 ms per episode; the disk library and retrieval still grow. Real-robot comparisons omit CLARE/DMPEL, and direct cross-embodiment transfer is unproven.

**Code and materials.** No verifiable official project/code link was found in the abstract or full paper.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24804v1)

## GPT-6 Astra Is a Native Visuo-Tactile Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation

- arXiv: [2610.26846v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26846v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-19 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** Tactile RGB images are supplied through a visual interface for contact-rich manipulation, together with few-shot demonstrations and interaction memory.

**Evidence and limitations.** Across 19 simulated tasks, the two benchmark means are 93.8% and 81.8%. Tactile input helps six tasks, ties nine, and hurts four. Most tasks have only 10 trials. External baselines reuse published numbers and Grasp Chip relaxes the success criterion, so this is not a strictly matched state-of-the-art comparison. Mean response time is 21.1 s; there is no real-robot or cross-sensor transfer evidence.

**Code and materials.** Benchmark adapters, evaluation tools, and replay tools were public.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26846v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/ccyydd/TacTalent) · [Project](https://tactalent.github.io/)

## SteerAct: Unlocking Action Models through Few-Shot Temporal Steering

- arXiv: [2610.25632v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25632v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-19 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** A per-task bank of 24 success/failure-labeled rollouts supplies temporal feature references. Online steering chooses intervention timing, direction, and strength while π0.5/Fast-WAM stay frozen. It does not update the main model weights at test time.

**Evidence and limitations.** Across three real YAM tasks with 30 trials per method per task, the Enc variant averages 52.2%, versus 31.1% for base π0.5 and 40.0% for the strongest comparison, DrVLA. Reference-count, feature-count, and gating ablations are provided. A shared SAE still requires training, features are selected per task, and labeled successes/failures are needed. The method is not entirely training-free, and cross-task cumulative memory is untested.

**Code and materials.** The project was accessible, but no verifiable code download was found.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25632v1) · [Project](https://steeract.github.io)

## PICA-WAM: Visual Perturbation Invariance via Cross-Appearance Self-Alignment in World Action Models

- arXiv: [2610.26813v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26813v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-19 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** An EMA teacher under normal appearance aligns shallow/deep action representations with a student receiving perturbed observations. No extra model is needed at inference.

**Evidence and limitations.** Evaluation covers four WAMs and three simulated benchmarks. On eight ARX-Lift2 grasping tasks with 160 trials per method, OpenWAM-α rises from 33.1% to 67.5%; π0.5 reaches 55.6%. For the strong InternW0-Δ base on LIBERO-Plus, improvement is only 92.8% to 93.3%. Real tests mainly concern appearance-perturbed grasping; long-horizon, dynamics, and cross-embodiment generalization are unproven.

**Code and materials.** The official repository contained documentation/resources only. Code and checkpoints were planned for November 2026 and were not yet available for reproduction.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26813v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/YudeZou/PICA-WAM-release)

## Self-Improving Video-to-Action Models

- arXiv: [2610.24177v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24177v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-16 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** A video planner proposes goal videos, and a video-to-action model generates video/action training pairs through its own interactions and improves iteratively.

**Evidence and limitations.** Across 24 DMC/OGBench/Atari tasks, OGBench success reaches 0.92 after 1M interactions, versus LAIfO at 0.63. Minecraft lava escape succeeds 76% with retained failure context versus 46% with cleared history. There are no real robots. The control-simulation video planner needs DAgger recovery data, and offline diamond results use expert action labels, so the overall work is not uniformly free of expert data.

**Code and materials.** The project stated Code Coming Soon; code and weights were not released.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24177v1) · [Project](https://wilcoxsonm21.github.io/mirror/)

## TrajWAM: An Efficient Trajectory-Guided World Action Model for Dynamic Robotic Manipulation

- arXiv: [2610.25763v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25763v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-19 UTC
- Review: Abstract and project tables checked; full-text retrieval unsuccessful

**Contribution and context.** Historical object masks predict trajectories; a Trajectory ControlNet guides future vision. Video distillation, adapter realignment, and action-rollout distillation preserve a one-step future-conditioned interface for dynamic manipulation.

**Evidence and limitations.** The project tables report 43.54% success over 35 DOMINO tasks at 108.5 ms inference latency. Across 480 real conveyor/turntable trials, success is 74.2% versus 49.2% for π0.5. Only the abstract and project experiment tables were checked: full-text retrieval failed, so training-budget fairness, mask acquisition, and ablations remain unverified. State-of-the-art claims are not treated as established conclusions.

**Code and materials.** The official repository contained only a README and explicitly stated Code coming soon.

## LIBERO-Pro2.0: A Benchmark for Evaluating the Robustness of Embodied Foundation Models

- arXiv: [2610.24587v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24587v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-17 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** The benchmark evaluates 17 policies, including VLAs and WAMs, using 22 static perturbation types, 20 runtime interventions, and 1,648 valid task variants. It probes feedback use, recovery, and combined perturbations.

**Evidence and limitations.** Runtime intervention reduces mean success by 26.5 percentage points. FastWAM leads π0.5 under standard conditions (97.8% versus 93.3%) but trails after perturbation (70.1% versus 73.8%). Shorter action chunks do not help monotonically; Retry can also reduce the average. All tests are simulated. A negative feedback differential means reduced benefit under perturbation, not that visual feedback is useless.

**Code and materials.** The official repository cited in the paper returned 404 at review time; code availability was unverified.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24587v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/LIBERO-Pro-Team/LIBERO-Pro2.0) · [Project](https://racingemperor.github.io/LIBERO-Pro2.0)

## LIBERO-Dynamic: Benchmarking Robotic Manipulation in Dynamic Environments

- arXiv: [2610.24579v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24579v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-17 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** Forty LIBERO tasks receive three trajectory types at three speed levels, with physics advancing during inference according to measured latency. Six policies each face 1,800 dynamic conditions, supported by 9,000 dynamic demonstrations.

**Evidence and limitations.** Synchronous, asynchronous, and RTC execution are compared with a fixed checkpoint. VLASH uses a different checkpoint and is a system-level reference only. Four real task categories show task-dependent benefits of execution policy and time horizon. Real dynamic demonstrations are used for training, so the tests are not direct sim-to-real transfer. Fixed calibrated latency and scripted trajectories omit jitter and unpredictable motion.

**Code and materials.** The paper supplied a project page, but access failed at review; code was unverified.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24579v1) · [Project](https://renshaojie233.github.io/LIBERO-Dynamic)

## DialogMem: Evaluating Dialogue-Grounded Embodied Memory in 3D Environments

- arXiv: [2610.24767v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24767v1) · New paper · First submitted: 2026-10-17 UTC
- Review: Selected full-text methods/experiments checked

**Contribution and context.** RGB-D, poses, and human dialogue are written to memory for instance navigation and question answering across 142 episodes, 27 scanned scenes, and nine memory representations. The diagnostic separates grounding, retrieval, and action localization.

**Evidence and limitations.** Adding dialogue to 3D-Mem improves QA from 35.1 to 63.4, but target-localization success only from 31.0 to 31.1. Whether memory supports action must be tested separately. Dialogue is LLM-generated from specifications and poses are noiseless. The main navigation metric is target localization, not real manipulation success. No new memory algorithm is introduced.

**Code and materials.** No official code/data link was found in the abstract or readable full text; release status was unverified.

[Paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24767v1)

**Evidence and limitations.** The abstract describes weaknesses in history-dependent recovery assessment and temporal consistency under zero-/one-shot, cross-environment, and cross-embodiment settings. ## Reading connections

Compile Once examines inheritance and selection controls; SteerAct examines success/failure reference gating; LIBERO-Pro2.0 examines runtime recovery. Together they frame whether experience is reusable, when intervention helps, and whether gains survive fair evaluation. SARAM supplies a parameterized continual-learning comparison, while DialogMem separates memory-question answering from action benefit.
