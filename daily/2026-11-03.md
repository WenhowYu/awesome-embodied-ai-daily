**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.00344v1 · New paper · First submitted 2026-10-30T20:24:55Z. Review: full-text

**Contribution:** SPARK uses a semantic critic, targeted physical revisits and selective merging to correct multi-episode embodied memory.
