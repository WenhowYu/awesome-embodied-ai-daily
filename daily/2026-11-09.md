### DexDICE: Reconciling Safety and Performance via Dual-Expert Offline Imitation Learning

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.09433v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-09 · First submitted 2026-11-06T13:04:30Z. Review: Official abstract, submission history and selected full-text methods, results and limitations; no independent reproduction.

**Contribution:** DexDICE separates safety-compliant and performance-oriented demonstrations, combining distribution correction with safe-support constraints. It evaluates 38 re-curated DSRL tasks and a physical mobile robot trained from teleoperation. The robot reached its goal in all 10 trials with no observed safety violation. This makes it directly relevant to learning robot policies from imperfect demonstrations.

**Limitations:** The zero-unsafe-visitation theorem concerns an ideal policy satisfying the support constraint exactly. Section 5.1 reports that fixed-κ DexDICE satisfies the cost constraint on 31 of 38 tasks. Ten physical trials in a two-obstacle arena do not establish deployment-wide safety.

**Resources checked:** Public repository verified; root contains dsrl_code/ and real_code/. Code not executed.

### Probably Safe Sim-to-Real Transfer with Lifetime Validity

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.09344v1 · New paper · Announcement 2026-11-09 · First submitted 2026-11-06T11:41:47Z. Review: Official abstract, submission history and selected full-text methods, results and limitations; no independent reproduction.

**Contribution:** Dejavu certifies each simulation-trained policy before deployment using only trajectories from a safe deployed baseline. Per-decision importance sampling, betting confidence sequences and summable failure budgets aim to preserve statistical validity across an unbounded sequence of promotions. **Limitations:** Guarantees assume bounded costs and controlled importance weights, independent reset trajectories, fixed initial-state distribution and fixed production dynamics. ## Coverage and exclusions

All 60 cs.RO new/cross-listed titles were screened: 54 primary new submissions and 6 cross-lists. This edition includes 38 new papers from that cohort, excludes 21 peripheral items, and deduplicates Open-HumanWAM (2611.06817): its v2 was submitted on November 5, so the November 9 cross-list is not a new version.

The November 9 new/cross-list title cohorts in cs.AI, cs.LG and cs.CV were screened with targeted abstract/full-text checks and catalog deduplication; 7 additional papers are included. Category cohorts overlap and should not be summed. AI/LG replacement cohorts outside the separately reviewed cs.RO/cs.CV feeds were not exhaustively screened.

The cs.RO/cs.CV replacement lists contain 43 and 93 entries, or 127 unique papers after removing 9 overlaps. A scoped review compared 41 selected pairs of immediately consecutive official full texts: 21 research/status updates are included, 15 pairs did not justify a substantive digest entry, and 5 verified changes were excluded as peripheral. The other 86 replacements were not selected for detailed full-text comparison. Extracted text, tables and captions were compared; figure-image binaries were not separately pixel-diffed.

LeWAM, FRED, BehaviorGraphs, NEO-VLA, STAR, VOMMI, Fast-TD-MPC, PhyProbe, CATS, NoContactNoWorries and Sketch-MoMa did not establish new substantive evidence in the checked versions. FlowSim and the recurrent-3D update-gate paper only added resource links; MoRE-GS had no changed scientific HTML after normalizing versioned URLs. DexHOT contains a narrow candidate-set equation correction, not a new empirical result, and is not labeled metadata-only.

RoboFQE and Spectral Alignment are evidence corrections, while geometric-prior pretraining materially narrows its claims. These 3 updates are distinguished from the 18 research/protocol revisions; old results are not presented as new progress. OpenView, CLOI, RiskFlow, shared canonical object frames and latent Riemannian flow were excluded for weak connection to the learning/memory/action scope. BEVCast and TAPF travel-cost calibration were also left out of the new-paper selection.

Simulation throughput, staged progress, conditional subtask success, pre-captured RGB-D replay and component latency are not interchangeable with closed-loop real-robot task success. Lodos/Lodos-v2 are supporting simulation infrastructure. Code/project availability is historical to this check; no experiments were reproduced.
