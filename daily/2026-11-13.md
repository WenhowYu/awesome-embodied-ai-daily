### Recover, Don't Just Continue: Learning from Feedback for Physical Failure Understanding and Correction

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.15651v1 · new · Announcement 2026-11-13 · First submitted 2026-11-11T16:01:28Z. Review: Official v1 full-text methods/results spot-check; not independently reproduced.

### JustRUA: A Controlled Study of GPT-6 Robot-Use Agents

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.16009v1 · new · Announcement 2026-11-13 · First submitted 2026-11-11T21:08:31Z. Review: Official v1 full-text methods/results spot-check; not independently reproduced.

**Contribution:** JustRUA controls four design axes for a GPT-6 Astra robot-use agent: harness, action interface, experience reuse, and geometry. The final stack retains episode history, short motion execution, grip feedback, RGB-predicted depth and code-based 3D coordinate solving.

**Evidence, changes and limits:** On the RoboDojo simulation evaluation set, score rises from 29.7 to 38.0 with full history and a 20-control-step cap per motion, and to 50.3 with the final configuration. Predicted depth reduces empty grasps from 41 to 16 in the first round.

**Limitations:** Score is not success rate; the final configuration also removes the decision cap. Demonstrations and the self-built skill library draw on scored tasks, cost more, and show no clear gain under this protocol; there is no hardware evaluation.

**Resources checked:** 2/2 author-linked resources returned HTTP 200; contents and reproducibility not audited.

### Remember What Changes the Odds: Critic-Gated Event Memory for Long-Horizon Manipulation

**Metadata:** arXiv 2611.16386v1 · new · Announcement 2026-11-13 · First submitted 2026-11-12T06:55:11Z. Review: Official v1 full-text methods/results spot-check; not independently reproduced.

**Contribution:** CGEM writes progress and failure memories when a critic predicts a large change in eventual success, dropping a failure record after its subtask is completed.

**Evidence, changes and limits:** In a ten-subtask diagnostic with exact success probabilities, CGEM succeeds in 88.0% versus 0.0% for a 36-step observation window and kinematic event memory; removing failure records gives 1.7%. In a separate real-data event detection test (87 held-out dual-arm episodes), the best reported learned critic reaches AUROC 0.86.

**Limitations:** Closed-loop gains use a synthetic diagnostic and an exact critic. The real data assess offline detection within eventually successful episodes, not real-world closed-loop improvement or cross-episode learning.
