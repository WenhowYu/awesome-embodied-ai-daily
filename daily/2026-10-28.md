### Program-Level Adversarial Co-Adaptive Self-Play for Robotic Security

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.36143v1 · New paper · First submitted Mon, 26 Oct 2026 18:49:21 UTC. Review: Official metadata and key full-text methods, experiments and limitations checked; results not reproduced.

**Contribution:** Coding agents revise executable attacks and defenses from opponent code and mission feedback, without model fine-tuning; adaptation is evaluated as program behavior rather than presumed cumulative robustness.

**Evidence:** One eight-generation lineage each in Autoware-CARLA and G1-MuJoCo. Across 40 driving matches, 18 reached the goal, 16 timed out and 6 collided; humanoid matches include clean, defense-only, attack-only and joint controls with three joint repeats per attacker.

**Limitations:** Simulation only, one lineage and simple task per configuration. Feedback mechanisms are not individually ablated; peak-speed/progress scores do not establish comprehensive safety, and local recovery can be defeated later.

**Resources checked:** Full text accessible; no associated implementation release was established from the inspected paper links.

### HIDRA: Hierarchical Dual-Routing Attention for Replay-Free Lifelong Imitation Learning

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.36374v1 · New paper · First submitted Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:43:30 UTC. Review: Official metadata and key full-text methods, experiments and limitations checked; results not reproduced.

**Contribution:** Replay-free lifelong imitation learning separates instruction-guided expert retrieval from context refinement, with key geometry regularization to reduce routing interference as experts expand.

**Evidence:** Across three seeds and 20 evaluation trials per seed, LIBERO-Object/Goal/Spatial/10 AUCs are 91.5/66.7/58.7/58.1 versus 82.2/64.3/47.0/53.8 for full-replay DMPEL. LIBERO-INS AUC is 27.6±3.8 versus 27.1±4.3, so the language-variation margin is small.

**Limitations:** AUC aggregates task success over continual-learning stages, not one final success rate. Evaluation is simulated LIBERO, initialized from shared pretrained policies; experts grow with tasks, spatial reasoning remains difficult, and real robots are future work.

### RoboAuto: Autonomous Robot Program Search through Multi-Level Physical Understanding

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.36533v1 · New paper · First submitted Tue, 27 Oct 2026 02:41:22 UTC. Review: Official metadata and key full-text methods, experiments and limitations checked; results not reproduced.

**Contribution:** RoboAuto combines executable skills and object-centric physical knowledge with scene grounding, stage verification and self-designed experiments for robot program search.

**Evidence:** LIBERO-PRO Long success is 73.4% versus ASPIRE 30.8%, and LIBERO-Plus 90.3% versus 44.7%. On three WidowX tasks, mean stage-completion scores are 100.00/100.00/95.83 versus ASPIRE 30.00/3.75/0.00 over 20 trials per task; both search methods receive 10 real search rollouts. Adding the knowledge base alone to ASPIRE improves three Spatial tasks but worsens another and does not solve the three Goal tasks.

**Limitations:** Real-robot figures are partial stage scores, not full-task success; humans reset, judge search outcomes and maintain safety. Real DROID π0.5 is used without task-specific search, so its zero score is not a matched adaptation-budget comparison. Several short-horizon/Robosuite baselines are imported published results; Long/Plus autonomous baselines are reproduced with matched coding models and disjoint search/test scenes.

**Resources checked:** Project page accessible and explicitly labels code as coming soon; implementation release not yet confirmed.

### Remember Where You Walk: Learning Spatial Memory for End-to-End Legged Robot Exploration

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.37203v1 · New paper · First submitted Tue, 27 Oct 2026 13:22:51 UTC. Review: Official metadata and key full-text methods, experiments and limitations checked; results not reproduced.

**Contribution:** Local Spatial Matrix Memory writes task-relevant information into position-indexed cells for end-to-end RL exploration, making retained spatial information measurable through reconstruction.

**Evidence:** Three seeds across 100 unseen mazes per architecture: exploration coverage remains comparable across memories (65.4–82.8%), while LSMM reconstruction error falls from about 0.33 to 0.10. **Limitations:** Real Go2 demonstration uses external AprilTag localization and premeasured wall positions to synthesize height observations, not onboard terrain perception. Memory assumes a fixed bounded coordinate grid; the deployment example is not a statistical real-world baseline comparison.

No matched adaptation budget makes standalone pi0.5 an incomplete causal control. Early iterations optimize 10 tasks before expanding to 42.

### The Trinity:Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.36077v1 · New paper · First submitted Mon, 26 Oct 2026 18:03:34 UTC. Review: Official metadata and selected full-text methods, results and limitations checked; not reproduced.

**Contribution:** The Trinity groups tasks by capability requirements and evolves reusable harness modules through a capability tree, combining generated verifiable environments, selective inheritance, experience retrieval and capability-aware routing around a frozen backbone.

**Evidence:** Benchmark-equal means rise from 39.06 to 73.16 on 28 training benchmarks and 37.45 to 63.61 on 18 unseen benchmarks. CaP-X real-robot success rises from 0% to 60% over 10 layouts/episodes; SOON real success also reaches 60%. Each harness gets five separate simulated skill-preparation episodes before evaluation. Under matched deployable-routing budgets, adding retrieved experience to capability-aware routing raises Unseen from 58.27 to 60.96 and lowers estimated total inference tokens from 556.21M to 541.28M (including preparation/routing). Relevant-module addition raises Train by 19.39 points versus 1.02 for an irrelevant module; removing a relevant evolved module costs 2.74 points.

**Limitations:** Headline means mix benchmark metrics and use oracle-compatible node selection, whereas practical self-routing is separately evaluated. Harnesses have their own prepared skills; deployments differ in interfaces, guidance and acceptance rules, so results do not isolate module evolution. CaP-X lifts cups in simulation but pushes them aside physically. Tiny robot samples and a fixed capability-tree topology limit generalization claims. Unseen memory starts from training records and receives verified feedback only for later instances; preparation records and evaluation capability labels are excluded. SOON uses one route and 10 episodes; these are not 10 distinct routes.

**Resources checked:** Project and repository read. Repository currently contains report/assets/overview only; README announces code for November 15, 2026, not a verified release.

### SO-101 Bench: Accessible Spatial Manipulation

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.36420v1 · New paper · First submitted 2026-10-27 00:02:28 UTC. Review: Official abstract metadata and selected full-text methods, experiments and limitations checked; reported results are author-reported, not independently reproduced.

**Contribution:** An Isaac Lab digital twin benchmarks spatial language-conditioned SO-101 manipulation across held-out object splits, with equal-data/compute policy adaptation, agent-harness ablations and same-weight sim-to-real tests.

**Evidence:** Nine learned VLA/WAM policies use 3,629 simulated demonstrations and a 36 h ×4 A100 fine-tuning budget. The primary score averages 15 task-family/split cells over 916 episodes: InternW0-Delta 61.4%, Galaxea 0.5 60.8%, and GPT-6.1 Sol/PyRUA-Lean plus Galaxea 0.5 83.2%. On 60 paired ablation episodes, the same Sol model succeeds in 21/60 with direct tools, 40/60 with code execution and 51/60 with VLA grasp hand-off, versus VLA alone 32/60. On 45 real layouts, hand-off succeeds 29/45 versus VLA 24/45. Five policies retain exact binary-success rankings (Spearman 1.00), but simulation overestimates success by 9–31 percentage points.

**Limitations:** Only one real environment and five policies validate ranking; MMRV 95% CI is [0,0.169]. Fixed compute leaves some policies under-trained, so scores are not capability ceilings.
