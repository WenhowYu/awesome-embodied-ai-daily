### Certified Behavioral Retention for Continual Adaptation with Held-Out Anchors

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.40740v1 · New paper · First submitted 2026-10-29T15:08:12Z. Review: full-text

**Contribution:** CABA gates candidate updates using held-out historical anchors and worst-anchor forward KL, choosing the largest tested interpolation within a declared retention budget.

**Evidence:** A three-seed UCI-HAR sensor-classification proxy reports forgetting 1.01→0.00 percentage points at budget 0.05, while final average accuracy falls 93.04%→88.48%. Conditional theory requires coverage and Lipschitz assumptions.

**Limitations:** No robot or foundation-model validation. Behavioral-distribution retention is not task accuracy, utility or global safety; frozen representations and compatible output distributions are required.

**Resources checked:** No independently verified code repository in this review.

**Metadata:** arXiv 2610.41157v1 · New paper · First submitted 2026-10-30. Review: Full-text methods, results and limitations checked; no reproduction.
